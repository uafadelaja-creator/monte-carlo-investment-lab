## Methodological improvement

- Added `monitor_stat_arb.py`, an idempotent aggregate monitoring ledger.

  series rather than silently contaminating prior evidence.
- Added a prior-prefix price hash check. The 2026-10-16 prefix did **not** match
  the previous download, disclosing an upstream adjusted-price revision. Aggregate
  strategy changes were economically negligible.
- Expanded summaries to show both 12- and 36-month profit/loss probabilities.
- Added exact CI pins, completed-session download defaults, bounded official-factor
  retries, and four monitoring tests. All eleven tests pass.

## Interpretation and limits

This weekly extension adds no evidence of implementable alpha. It remains severely
underpowered, survivor-selected, and dependent on vendor-adjusted closes rather
than executable prices. Borrow availability, locates, spreads, market impact,
margin, taxes, and delistings remain unobserved. Repeated weekly checks are
dependent monitoring—not new independent statistical tests.

## Next concrete improvement

Preserve the frozen ledger while adding a separate forward-only paper-execution
log with timestamped signals, next-session open/close audit prices, observed
spread proxies, and explicit borrow-availability status. That work must remain
research-only until enough independent events exist.
