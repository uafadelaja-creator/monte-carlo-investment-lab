## Actual versus assumed evidence

- **Observed:** vendor-adjusted daily histories, raw daily audit bars/volume,
  fixed-code holdout returns, recorded scanner snapshots, and official factor data.
- **Assumed:** adjusted-close delayed fills, 10 bps turnover cost, 3% borrow drag,
  available short inventory, and repeatability of moving-block resamples.
- **Unavailable:** quote-level spreads, contemporaneous locates and borrow rates,
  delisting-inclusive point-in-time membership, CRSP access, and a credible
  date-known company-classification panel. No speculative-versus-proven stock
  conclusion is made.
