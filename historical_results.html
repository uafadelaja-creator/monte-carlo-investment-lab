From prices to possible outcomes

Methodology
01

Start with observed prices

The uploaded CSV contains adjusted daily closes for ARKK, QUAL, and SPY. Dates must be unique and prices finite, positive, and complete.

02

Use a common monthly history

Take each completed month's final price and calculate percentage changes. Exclude partial November and reject missing months.

03

Resample market periods together

Draw consecutive three-month blocks of all three ETFs' returns. Repeat to generate 50,000 paths of up to 36 months.

04

Apply deposits and compare risk

Each arm starts with $500. Weekly contributions are converted to month-end deposits. Drawdowns exclude deposits so new cash does not hide losses.

What the model does not establish

  • ETF proxies are not survivorship-free point-in-time stock cohorts.
  • The sample begins in 2015 and is highly regime-dependent.
  • Moving-block bootstrap resamples observed history and does not forecast.
  • Correlation and volatility can change abruptly outside the sample.
  • Month-end data miss intramonth drawdowns and liquidity gaps.
  • Adjusted-price histories may be revised by the data vendor.
  • Fund operating expenses are embedded in ETF history; no additional fee is deducted. Taxes, trading costs, inflation, and exact weekly timing are omitted.
  • A fixed zero-percent risk-free rate is used only for transparent Sharpe comparison.

This is descriptive risk research, not evidence of a profitable trading strategy or a validated forecast. No out-of-sample validation is claimed.

Stress assumptions

The optional shock is hypothetical, not an observed event or estimated probability.
