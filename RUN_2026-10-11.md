# Monte Carlo run: 11 October 2026

## Executive finding

The project now has a completed empirical block-bootstrap model rather than only a historically anchored parametric demonstration. The model resamples 140 common monthly ARKK, QUAL, and SPY returns from February 2015 through September 2026 using 50,000 joint paths and a fixed seed.

ARKK generated the widest upside range and beat its paired SPY path 53.2% of the time at 36 months, but this is too close to chance to demonstrate an edge. Its historical month-end maximum drawdown was 77.1%, versus 27.8% QUAL and 23.9% SPY.

## Robustness

Changing the block length from one to three to six months produced ARKK median values of $20,383, $20,264, and $20,658. QUAL medians were $19,712, $19,715, and $19,724; SPY medians were $19,908, $19,926, and $19,918. Central wealth estimates are therefore not highly sensitive to these tested block lengths, although loss probabilities move several percentage points.

The hypothetical first-month shock lowers 36-month medians to $20,075 ARKK, $19,605 QUAL, and $19,791 SPY. Because later contributions dilute a one-time early shock, drawdown distributions carry more information than ending medians: shocked median drawdowns rise to 42.9%, 19.3%, and 20.9%.
