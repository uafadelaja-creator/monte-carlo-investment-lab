## Forward audit

`forward_signal_audit.py` now starts a prospective, append-only evidence series.
It does not reconstruct earlier observations as if they had been recorded live.

| KO/PEP | 2026-10-30 | Flat | unavailable | unavailable | No |
| XOM/CVX | 2026-10-30 | Flat | −0.60 | 0.071 | No |

Every ledger row is chained to the prior SHA-256. A future entry observation can
only be resolved on a later run after the next completed session is available.
Raw opens/closes, daily ranges, and trailing dollar volume are stored as audit
context. Daily range is not a bid/ask spread, and borrow availability remains
unobserved.

## Interpretation and next improvement

This run adds no evidence of implementable alpha. The study remains sparse,
survivor-selected, and dependent on vendor-adjusted daily data. The original
assumption-driven contribution model remains a separate illustrative engine and
is not evidence of historical edge.

The next concrete improvement is a documented bid/ask or trade-and-quote source
plus contemporaneous borrow inventory. Until that is accessible, the project will
continue to label daily range and the 3% borrow charge as proxies or scenarios.
