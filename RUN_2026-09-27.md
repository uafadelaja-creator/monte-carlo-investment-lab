# Monte Carlo run: 27 September 2026

This v0.2 run uses 50,000 paired paths, seed 20260927, a $500 starting balance, and the month-end equivalent of $100 weekly contributions. Total contributions at 36 months are $16,100. ARKK, QUAL, and SPY are transparent proxies for speculative technology, established quality companies, and a diversified U.S. index.

The speculative proxy has the widest upside range, but its lower median and much deeper drawdown distribution show that volatility can overwhelm a strong historical return anchor. QUAL and SPY produce similar central outcomes; SPY has the highest mean and median at 36 months.

These are conditional scenario outputs, not forecasts or evidence of an edge. Limitations include ETF-proxy survivorship, regime dependence, unstable correlations, assumed tail shape, constant parameters, monthly contribution approximation, and exclusion of taxes, inflation, and trading costs.

Resume bullet: Built a reproducible Python Monte Carlo investment lab using 50,000 correlated, fat-tailed paths to compare speculative technology, quality-company, and S&P 500 proxy portfolios across six horizons; separated contributions from gains and quantified percentile wealth, loss probability, benchmark outperformance, CAGR, volatility, and drawdown risk.
