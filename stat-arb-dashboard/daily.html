How the scan operates

Stock combinations are rebuilt each run from peer-sector groups. For each current, well-covered pair, the latest 252 completed formation closes estimate a log-price hedge relationship, excluding the observation day. A 90-session return correlation and a 60-residual standardized deviation are computed. Correlation is used to skip expensive cointegration tests for weakly related pairs; skipped comparisons remain in the false-discovery correction as p = 1.

Quality controls and limitations

The scanner rejects missing, inconsistent or stale feeds rather than publishing a false refresh. Adjusted price history is obtained from a free Yahoo data feed, not live exchange-verified quotes. The source and all hypotheses are exploratory, and multiple-testing correction does not solve overfitting or establish financial profitability. Days without a market session may have the previous closing date.
