## Methodological change and validation

- Added Monte Carlo standard errors and Wilson 95% intervals to every profitable-
  and loss-frequency estimate at all 1/3/6/12/24/36-month horizons.
- Exposed 12- and 36-month loss intervals in the aggregate CSV and report.
- Added boundary and invariant tests so zero-event and all-event simulations retain
  valid intervals.
- Validation: 17 unit tests pass. A second 50,000-path execution using the same
  inputs and seed reproduced all pair reports, bootstrap tables, aggregate summary,
  sensitivity table, and Markdown report byte-for-byte. Re-appending both ledgers
  was idempotent.

These confidence intervals isolate finite-path simulation noise only. They do not
include uncertainty from model choice, sparse entries, historical regimes, vendor
revisions, unknown bid/ask spreads, unobserved borrow inventory, or data mining.

## Actual versus assumed evidence

- **Observed:** vendor-adjusted daily histories, raw daily audit bars/volume,
  realized holdout returns under the code, and official factor history.
- **Assumed:** delayed fills at adjusted closes, 10 bps turnover cost, 3% borrow
  drag, available short inventory, and repeatability of moving-block resamples.
- **Not available:** trade-and-quote spreads, contemporaneous locates/borrow rates,
  delisting-inclusive point-in-time membership, and a credible date-known company
  classification panel. No speculative-versus-proven stock conclusion is made.

## Interpretation and next improvement

The frozen study still provides no evidence of implementable statistical-arbitrage
alpha. Three historical entries and zero prospective entries are underpowered.
The next concrete improvement is to add a full synthetic lifecycle test for a
prospective entry resolving exactly once on the next completed session, while
continuing to seek authorized quote/borrow data rather than inferring execution
quality from daily bars.
