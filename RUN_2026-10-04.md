# Monte Carlo run: 4 October 2026

This v0.3 run uses market information available through the 2 October 2026 close, 50,000 paired paths, seed 20261004, a $500 initial investment, and the month-end equivalent of $100 weekly contributions. Total contributions at 36 months are $16,100.

ARKK's right tail pulls its mean above its median, but its loss and drawdown risks remain much larger. A 51.3% paired probability of beating SPY is indistinguishable from a useful edge without model-parameter uncertainty and out-of-sample validation. QUAL and SPY have similar risk, while SPY retains the stronger central result under the chosen historical anchors.

The stress case can have a higher mean or median than the simple return-haircut case because lognormal convexity increases arithmetic wealth when log-return volatility rises. That is a model effect, not a claim that volatility creates value; the sharply worse 5th percentile and drawdown metrics are the decision-relevant risk signals.
