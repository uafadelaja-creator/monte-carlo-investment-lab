# Research report — suggested final publication format

The **Research report** tab and CLI generate a report directly from real observed adjusted-price data. This file is a narrative drafting scaffold only; it contains no invented performance figures.

## Report header
- Date generated
- Common market-data period and provider
- Dataset SHA-256 / analysis commit
- Dated Shariah holdings evidence and methodology

## 1. Executive summary
What is the research question? Are results descriptive, or a historical investment strategy? What cannot be concluded?

## 2. Historical ETF performance
Paste metrics from the actual dashboard: CAGR, volatility, Sharpe/Sortino, **monthly** drawdowns, and tracking error versus SPY.

## 3. Portfolio construction and concentration
Compare equal-weight, top-ETF-holding-proportional, minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe approaches. Describe how the year-2026 holdings universe restricts the historical claims.

## 4. Cross-validation and sensitivity
Show rolling 12/36/60-month returns, SPY stress-month comparisons, and walk-forward weighting tests. Highlight remaining constituent-selection hindsight bias.

## 5. Scenario analysis
State horizons, block lengths, path counts, initial and monthly cash contributions, random seed, paired resampling policy. Report only actual computed percentiles and conditional outcome frequencies.

## 6. Shariah/compliance limitations
Distinguish business + financial screening, issuer inclusion, independent stock-level approvals and dividend purification. Do not convert a 2026 holdings list into historic eligibility.

## 7. Conclusion and next work
Discuss whether differences appear due to concentration, industry weights or alternative screening styles; distinguish data-supported observations from hypotheses.
